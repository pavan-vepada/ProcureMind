PROCURE MIND · CATEGORY BUYER JOURNEY

One sourcing event, from a plant's request to deliveries

Pavan buys packaging for Sunrise Consumer Goods. Follow one sourcing event through seven phases and four worlds: the plant, Pavan's desk inside Procure Mind, the suppliers, and leadership. Most of the journey happens outside any software; Procure Mind takes over the stretch that used to eat days.

🧑‍💼What Pavan does
  • Reads the plant's email and volume sheet
  • Asks 12 box makers about capacity and certificates (RFI)
  • Shortlists 5 and reads how each would do the job (RFP)
  • 🔐 Logs in and sees every event at its stage
  • Clicks Start RFx on the plant's request
  • Checks the draft and fixes one quantity
  • Ticks the must-have certificates, sets a pass score, clicks Lock rules & send
  • Answers supplier calls and questions
  • Gets on with other sourcing events
  • Watches the replies being read
  • Opens the Comparison
  • Clicks a price that came from a phone photo to check it
  • Accepts the easy items in one click
  • Clicks Ask supplier on the supplier who skipped 3 items
  • Reads the drafted email and clicks Send
  • Asks: "What if we combine both plants' volumes?"
  • Asks a follow-up and saves it as a scenario
  • Clicks Recommend award, then Download PDF
  • Presents the memo to leadership
  • Clicks any figure when finance asks "where is this from?"
  • Raises the purchase orders in the ERP
💻What Procure Mind does
  • Shows the request under Requests waiting for you, ready to start in one click
  • Drafts items, quantities per plant, questionnaire, terms and cover email
  • Checks every quantity against the volume sheet
  • Locks the rules with a timestamp, before any price is seen
  • Shows who has replied and who hasn't
  • Marks late replies
  • Reads every reply in under a minute, all at once
  • Converts every price to ₹ per piece, before tax, delivered
  • Outlines where each number came from
  • Flags only what needs a look
  • Drafts one email covering every gap
  • Reads the supplier's answer automatically
  • Updates only the 3 missing prices and keeps the old values
  • Turns each question into an exact calculation, done by code
  • Explains it in two sentences and shows the working
  • Builds the memo: savings, risks, evidence for every figure
  • The memo is the hand-off: every rupee links to the supplier's own words
